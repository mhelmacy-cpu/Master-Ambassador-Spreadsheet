Paste rows copied straight from a spreadsheet (tab-separated) or comma-separated, one ambassador per line, in this column order:

Name, Grade, Borough, Parent 1 Name, Parent 1 Email, Parent 2 Name, Parent 2 Email

Extra trailing columns are ignored. A student who already exists (matched by name + borough) gets updated in place; everyone else is added. Fields you've hand-edited on the sheet — Teacher, Student Email, Active, Notes — are never touched by an import.
